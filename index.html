About the project

World Mechanics is an independent research project on the use of artificial intelligence as an instrument for describing complex physical and biological systems — while remaining clear about the boundary between machine-generated representations and human understanding.

A machine may model, describe, predict, and simulate. Description is not consciousness.

Research focus

How far can a machine describe physical reality? Where does prediction end and understanding begin? How may computation assist quantum and life-science work without a confusion of categories? How should an engineer distinguish capacity from cognition?

The method is that of the platform:

Build → Deploy → Observe → Test → Learn → Repeat
