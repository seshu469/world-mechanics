01 / Thesis

A model can describe part of the world without becoming the world it describes.

World Mechanics treats AI as a scientific instrument: powerful when calibrated, testable and reproducible; unreliable when fluency is mistaken for evidence. The project asks what AI can represent, predict, control and explain — while keeping those claims separate from first-person subjective experience.

Prediction ≠ explanation ≠ reasoning ≠ understanding ≠ consciousness.
02 / Five-level framework

Five different claims. One discipline: never collapse them into one.

LEVEL 01

Representation

Preserve useful structure from data in an encoding or learned state.

LEVEL 02

Prediction

Generalise to new observations, cases or scientific targets.

LEVEL 03

Intervention

Select actions that reliably change a target system in predicted ways.

LEVEL 04

Mechanism

Expose computation or causal structure that can be independently tested.

LEVEL 05

Experience

Subjective first-person experience. Current research offers indicators, not a decisive operational test for AI consciousness.

Evidence for Levels 1–4 does not logically establish Level 5.
03 / Research domains

Where computation meets difficult scientific systems.

Machine Intelligence

Inside the model

Mechanistic interpretability, internal representation, reasoning behaviour, hallucination, grounding and the difference between an answer and the mechanism that produced it.

AI × Life Sciences

The language of biology

Biomolecular structure, genomic foundation models, cell representations and autonomous analysis — evaluated against experimental or quantitative targets.

AI × Quantum Systems

Learning difficult state spaces

Machine learning for quantum properties, experimental data, state representation, device control and optimisation without anthropomorphic claims.

06 / Research method

Build → test → observe → reproduce → revise.

Evidence

Claims follow sources

Primary peer-reviewed research is preferred. Preprints and laboratory reports are labelled. Established findings, interpretation and speculation are kept distinct.

Reproducibility

Research is infrastructure

Code, environments, data provenance, random seeds, checksums and evaluation outputs should be versioned wherever possible.

Secular research

No required worldview

No religious or anti-religious conclusion is assumed. Scientific claims depend on evidence, mathematics, testability and explicit uncertainty.
